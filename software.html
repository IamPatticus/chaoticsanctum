Headache Log

An offline-first headache and migraine tracker built to make consistency easier. Headache Log lets you quickly capture when a headache starts, how severe it feels, likely triggers, treatments you tried, and how recovery unfolded, so you can spot patterns without fighting the app itself.

Offline-first Private by default Simple entry flow Pattern tracking Cross-platform
Android release Windows download Web version Privacy policy
Why it exists Health tracking is only useful if it is easy enough to keep using on a bad day. Headache Log is designed around low-friction logging, not dashboards for their own sake.
Best fit People managing migraines, recurring headaches, and trigger discovery who want something practical, local-first, and not overly clinical.
